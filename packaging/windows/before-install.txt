Omega Trader — please read before installing
============================================

WHAT THIS IS
    A multi-indicator Forex trading bot with a web dashboard. It can run a
    backtest on historical data, trade on paper, or trade a live
    MetaTrader 5 account.

WHAT IT IS NOT
    A money machine. Measured on 16 years of real data across seven currency
    pairs, this strategy's out-of-sample expectancy is approximately zero.
    The numbers, including the ones that are unflattering, are in
    BACKTEST.md inside the install folder. Read it before risking anything.

AFTER INSTALLING
    The app starts in PAPER mode with simulated data, so the first run is
    safe and needs no broker. Your settings live in:

        %LOCALAPPDATA%\OmegaTrader\config.yaml

    Live trading requires a MetaTrader 5 terminal installed and running on
    this machine, your account details in that file, and explicitly setting
    execution.confirm_live to true. That second lock exists on purpose.

RISK
    Trading leveraged foreign exchange can lose you more than you deposit.
